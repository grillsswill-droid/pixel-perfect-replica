# Grill & Swill customer storefront

Mode: Persuade

Audience: hungry customers choosing delivery or pickup, usually on a phone. The job is to understand the offer immediately, see real meals and prices, and start an order without losing trust in availability, customization, or totals.

Constraints: preserve the official mark, real product data, Quick Order immediately after the hero, guest checkout, USD pricing, existing routes, and the cream/charcoal/ember identity. Do not invent claims or testimonials.

## Direction contract

THESIS: “Ember Fold Service” makes the storefront feel like opening Grill & Swill’s own branded takeaway wrap: food, choice, and fulfillment unfold in one continuous journey. It refuses the category-default centered headline followed by a row of interchangeable cards.

OWN-WORLD: Warm cream fiber-paper fields, crisp white food surfaces, charcoal ink, and ember-orange stamps form the entire system. Fine crease rules organize content; bold Archivo headlines and Figtree body copy stay direct. The official flame-and-ampersand mark remains untouched. Status colors are limited to semantic use.

STORY: The first view creates appetite and makes ordering obvious. The next fold is Quick Order, grouped around real meal missions. Deeper folds explain customization, fulfillment, rewards, and operational confidence without delaying the menu.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact header sits above a full-width cream-paper hero. A large branded feast photograph occupies the right and breaks the fold line; the left holds one short headline, proof in menu specifics, delivery/pickup context, and the primary Start your order action. The lower crease visibly opens into Quick Order category controls so the next action is already in view.

FORM: Sixth grounded direction, a branded takeaway-wrapper fold sequence translated from one continuous sheet into page structure and state transitions. SEED REFERENCE seed:57615ddd5e1c05b6. Signature interaction: category changes and cart confirmation fold/reveal content along existing crease lines, using transform and opacity only.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
