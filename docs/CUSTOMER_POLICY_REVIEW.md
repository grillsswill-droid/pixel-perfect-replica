# Customer policy and help content review

Review/version date: **26 November 2026** (`2026-11-26`). This document records the factual basis, proposed coverage and owner decisions for customer-facing privacy, purchase, cancellation, delivery, food, storage, accessibility, FAQ and support pages. It does not change application behaviour or replace the security remediation plan.

## Scope and publication approach

Use the existing Grill & Swill brand name, guest checkout, hosted Flutterwave payment and public menu. Write plain customer instructions, with links to the real support route rather than an invented mailbox. Do not publish a company registration number, legal name, postal address, governing law, licence, food-safety certification, compliance certification, refund deadline or response-time promise without owner confirmation. Keep unconfirmed operational details in this internal review, not as bracketed placeholders in customer pages.

A policy can explain a request process without promising that a request is automatically accepted or fulfilled. Support acknowledgements must distinguish recording a request from emailing it, and email delivery from a staff reply. Customer-facing pages should give a clear support link and version date, but should not expose passcodes, database details, raw telemetry, internal audit findings or payment credentials.

## Drafting safeguards

- Retain required consumer/privacy rights without asserting that a particular country's law governs the service. Avoid broad liability exclusions, indemnity or dispute restrictions while operator/jurisdiction are unknown.
- Use “request”, “review” and “confirmation” precisely. Avoid “refund processed”, “email sent”, “order cancelled”, “booking confirmed” or “allergen-free” unless the respective action has actually occurred.
- Make changing details practical: ask for the order number/date and requested change; do not ask customers to post private tracking links in public comments or send complete payment credentials.
- Distinguish points and promo terms from implementation guarantees. Existing checkout settings are authoritative; do not advertise fixed rates where settings can change.
- Describe browser identifiers as device-linked information, not anonymous personal data. Explain both browser removal and retained server records.
- Separate customer requests from an emergency channel. Immediate medical danger belongs with local emergency services; a website message is not medical assistance.
- Policy version date must be shared across pages. New policy changes should not purport to erase mandatory rights or retroactively override completed orders.
- Cross-link related policy/help/support pages with real routes. Do not publish invented email/social accounts or display unresolved internal placeholders in the live page.
